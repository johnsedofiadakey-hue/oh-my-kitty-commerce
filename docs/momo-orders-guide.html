WhatsApp & MoMo Orders

How to record an order that was paid straight into your MoMo — and how to answer a customer who says they paid but never received it.

Who this is for — the owner, and anyone who takes orders on WhatsApp or Instagram.

Where to find it — the till (POS), and Orders in the admin menu.

Version — October 2026

Section 1The problem this solves

Orders placed on the website look after themselves. The money goes through the payment provider, and every order has a record attached to it.

Orders that come in by WhatsApp, Instagram or a phone call are different. The customer sends money straight to your MoMo number, you send the goods, and nothing is written down anywhere.

Weeks later a customer sends a screenshot of a payment and says the order never arrived. There is no way to tell whether that payment was for an order you already delivered, or a different order, or whether they are simply trying their luck. With no record, the safest thing feels like sending the goods again — so you pay twice.

What changes

Every outside order now gets written down at the moment you take it: what was bought, who paid, from which number, into which of your numbers, and — once it goes out — who packed it, who carried it, and when it arrived.

Be honest about what this is not

The system cannot check with MTN or Telecel whether a payment is real. No shop can do that for a personal MoMo number. What it does is keep a record good enough that a screenshot can be matched against it in seconds.

Section 2Recording the order

Open the till (POS) exactly as you would for a customer standing in front of you. At the top of the payment panel there are now two choices.

At the counterCustomer is here, takes it now
WhatsApp orderAlready paid by MoMo, needs delivery

Pick WhatsApp order. That one change is what keeps the order in the packing queue instead of treating it as finished.

1

Tap the products, same as always

The grid and the cart work exactly as they do for a counter sale.

2

Fill in the customer

The phone number is required for these orders — it is how the customer gets their order number by text, and that text is what you both refer back to if there is ever an argument. The delivery address is required too.

3

Record the payment

Paid into — which of your numbers the money landed in. Paid from — the number it came from, which is often not the same as the customer's own number. MoMo transaction ID — the reference on their screenshot.

4

Add the delivery fee, then tap Record order

The customer is texted their order number and a tracking link straight away.

The transaction ID is the important one

You can record an order without it — the sale is never blocked because a screenshot is blurry. But it is the single thing that lets you prove later which order a payment belongs to. Type it in whenever you can see it.

Section 3If the payment has been used before

When you type a transaction ID that has already paid for another order, the till stops and tells you which one:

What you will see

"That payment reference already paid for order OMK-G5CRX9KE. Check that order before recording another sale against it."

This is the moment a reused screenshot gets caught. Go and look at that order first.

If it was delivered

The customer has already had those goods. The order will show who packed it and when it arrived.

If they genuinely paid twice

Tap I checked — record it anyway. Your cart is kept, so nothing is lost.

Do not tap through it out of habit

The button exists for the rare case of a real double payment. If it becomes the normal thing to press, the check stops protecting you.

Section 4Packing and sending

A WhatsApp order does not count as delivered just because it is paid for. It goes to Dispatch and waits with everything else that needs packing.

To packRecorded, paid
PackedIn a parcel
OutWith the rider
DeliveredArrived

Each of those steps records who did it and when. That record is the thing that answers a dispute — so the habit that matters is marking parcels delivered as they arrive, and marking returns as they come back.

Why it matters

An order stuck at "Out" forever tells you nothing a month later. One marked delivered on a date is an answer.

If nothing comes back

The search will say so plainly: no order in the book was paid for with that reference. Either it was never recorded, or the payment was not for you.

Section 6Setting up your MoMo numbers

Before anyone records their first order, put your numbers in. Go to Content & Media and find "MoMo numbers money is received into".

One number per line, written as a label, a vertical bar, then the number:

Personal MoMo | 0241448231
Shop till | 0557778888

These become the list staff pick from. Keeping it a list rather than free typing is what lets you later total up what came into each number and check it against that number's own statement.

Worth doing at the same time

The text the customer receives is under the same screen, as "SMS: WhatsApp/MoMo order recorded". Change the wording to sound like you.

Section 7Questions you may have

Does this check with MTN whether the payment is real?

No, and nothing could, for a personal MoMo number. Only registered merchant accounts get that. What this does is record the payment well enough that you can match a screenshot against it.

What about orders from before this existed?

They cannot be recovered — there is nothing to recover them from. This starts from the day you begin using it.

Can a customer edit a screenshot?

Yes. That is exactly why the transaction ID matters: a faked reference will not match anything, and a real one that has already been used will be caught.

Can staff do this, or only me?

Staff can record these orders. They still cannot mark parcels returned — that stays with you, because marking a parcel returned is what would let the same order be packed again.

Does it still work if the internet drops?

Counter sales queue up offline as before. WhatsApp orders need a connection, because checking whether a transaction ID has been used before means reading what is already recorded.

Is there a way to stop this problem entirely?

Over time, yes. If outside customers paid through a payment link instead of sending to your personal number, every payment would record itself and none of this typing would be needed. Worth discussing when you have the time.
